# Specification: Recipe Deck Navigation via Overlapping Index Tabs

## Overview & Architecture Decision
In the Bread Lab mobile application, recipes evolve through experimental iterations branching from an original foundational formula (the "Master Formula"). 

After evaluating two physical metaphors for presenting iterated formulas, **Overlapping Index Tabs** pattern has been explicitly selected as the primary UX pattern.

This document details the architectural rationale, interaction specifications, component structure, and styling rules for implementing this pattern.

---

## Why Index Tabs Over Fanned Decks
1. **Ergonomic 1-Tap Switching**:
   - The fanned deck requires an initial tap to "spread" or expand the lineage deck, followed by a second interaction to inspect or select a specific version.
   - Index tabs expose all available versions (`Master`, `v1`, `v2`) directly at the top of the card. A baker with flour on their hands can swap directly between iterations in a single tap without layout jumps or accordion animations.
2. **Zero Gesture Ambiguity**:
   - Swiping or fanning cards vertically or horizontally often intercepts parent scroll gestures in a recipe list view. Tabs are standard, predictable touch targets.
3. **Continuous Context & Predictable Card Footprint**:
   - The card maintains a steady, compact vertical footprint in the feed list while still conveying full version history.

---

## Display Rules & Business Logic

### 1. Visible Tab Limit (Rule of 3)
- **Maximum visible tabs**: 3 tabs across the top edge of any iterated recipe card:
  1. `Master` (The baseline formula, always positioned leftmost).
  2. `v[N-1]` (The second most recent iteration, e.g., `v1 · Aug 10`).
  3. `v[N] (Latest)` (The active / latest iteration, e.g., `v2 (Latest) · Aug 21`, positioned rightmost).
- **Older Archived Batches**:
  - If a recipe has 4 or more iterations, older versions do not clutter the top tab row. They are accessible via an understated `+ N older archived iterations` link or dropdown drawer inside the card or review log.

### 2. Untouched / Non-Iterated Master Formulas
- If a recipe has no iterations (e.g., *Chocolate Molasses*):
  - Do **not** render top tabs.
  - Render a clean, standalone card with an editorial `Master Formula · Untouched` badge.

### 3. Tab Visual Hierarchy & States
- **Active Tab**:
  - Filled surface matching the card background (`#ffffff` or card container).
  - Merges seamlessly with the top edge of the card body (border-bottom disappears or matches card fill).
  - Includes a leading indicator dot (`•`) or bolded label (e.g., `• v2 (Latest) · Aug 21`).
  - Font: `HankenGrotesk_600SemiBold`, 12px.
- **Inactive / Background Tabs**:
  - Slightly recessed surface tone (e.g., `#fbf2ed` or `#e1d8d4` border/fill).
  - Explicit top-rounded tab shape with subtle border outlining.
  - Muted text tone (`#82736b`).
  - Clear icon glyphs:
    - Master tab: Shield icon (`shield-outline` or `bookmark`).
    - Prior iteration tab: Clock / history icon (`history` or `time-outline`).

## Component Layout & Content Specs

Each card consists of three core visual blocks:

### 1. Tab Bar (`IndexTabBar`)
Placed immediately above the card body with negative bottom margin or border-collapse so the active tab visually joins the card.
```
┌── [ 🛡️ Master ] ── [ 🕒 v1 · Aug 10 ] ── [ • v2 (Latest) · Aug 21 ] ──┐
```

### 2. Card Header & Baseline Meta
- **Lineage Chip**: `BASELINE V1.6 · 3 Iterations` (uppercase, tracked, 10px).
- **Title**: `[Recipe Name] (Iterated v2)` in `LibreCaslonText_700Bold`, 20px–22px.
- **Thumbnail Image**: Right-aligned 72x72 rounded preview thumbnail.

### 3. Scientific Data & Delta Highlights
- **Key Metrics Row**:
  - Hydration % (e.g., `62%`)
  - Starter / Levain % (e.g., `14%`)
  - Yield / Mass (e.g., `1 Loaf` or `850g`)
- **Delta vs. Master Callout (Crucial)**:
  - An accented callout banner directly beneath the metrics highlighting experimental changes:
  - *Example*: `Delta vs Master: +4% hydration for a more open crumb; 30 min extended autolyse stage.`
- **Card Footer**:
  - Left: Time elapsed since bake (e.g., `🕒 Baked 3 days ago`).
  - Right: Primary action link (e.g., `Review Log ›` or `Open Formulation ›`).

## Summary for the Engineering Agent
When generating, editing, or rendering recipe cards in the Bread Lab:
- Use **Option 1 (Overlapping Index Tabs)** as the canonical presentation for iterated formulas.
- Never use a carousel or gesture-heavy fanned card stack for recipe iterations.
- Adhere strictly to the Rule of 3 (Master + two latest iterations).
- Ensure the Delta Callout is always rendered on iterated cards to preserve the "Baker into Scientist" experimental feedback loop.
